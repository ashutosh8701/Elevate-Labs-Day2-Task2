# Task 2 — Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Analysed **9,994 transactions** totalling **$2.30M in sales** and **$286.4K in profit** — a **12.5% blended margin**.
- Discounts above **20% destroyed $135.4K in profit**, while orders at or below 20% generated **$421.8K** — establishing a clear policy threshold.
- Margin by discount band:
  - **0% → 29.5%**
  - **10–20% → 11.6%**
  - **20–30% → −10.1%**
  - **50%+ → −119.2%**
- **Furniture** was the margin outlier at **2.5%**, compared with **Technology at 17.4%** and **Office Supplies at 17.0%**.
- **Technology** generated the highest profit (**$145.5K**) despite Office Supplies selling comparable volume.
- **West region** led on both sales (**$725K**) and profit (**$108K**).
- **Consumer segment** contributed **$1.16M**, the highest of the three segments.
- **November and December** alone accounted for **29.5% of annual sales**.

## 🎯 Objective

Create interactive visualisations and derive meaningful business insights from sales data using **Power BI**.

- **Superstore Sales Dataset**
- **9,994 transactions**
- **21 fields**

## 🛠️ Tools Used

## 🔬 Work Performed

- Imported and transformed the Superstore dataset in Power BI.
- Created KPI cards for:
  - Total Sales
  - Total Profit
  - Quantity Sold
- Designed dashboards analysing:
  - Sales vs. profit by year and month
  - Profit by category and sub-category
  - Regional performance
  - Segment and ship-mode breakdowns
  - Discount impact on profit
- Quantified the discount threshold at which orders become loss-making.
- Applied **data storytelling techniques** to surface actionable business insights.
